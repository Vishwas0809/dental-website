Dr. Shivangi Sharma is a qualified BDS dentist with over 6.5 years of clinical experience and the founder of Bhuvneshwari Dental Clinic & Implant Centre in Haridwar, Uttarakhand.
She established the clinic with one clear goal: to bring calm, modern, and honest dental care to patients in Haridwar and surrounding areas including Rishikesh, Roorkee, and BHEL township.
Her clinical focus areas include:
	•	Dental Implants
	•	Painless Root Canal Treatment
	•	Smile Design and Cosmetic Dentistry
	•	Pediatric and Family Dentistry
	•	Crowns, Bridges, and Dentures
	•	Emergency Dental Care
Dr. Sharma is known for her patient-first approach — she takes time to explain treatment options clearly before proceeding, making her particularly popular with first-time patients and those who are nervous about dental visits.
Bhuvneshwari Dental Clinic is open Monday to Saturday 9 AM to 8 PM and Sunday 10 AM to 2 PM at Mamgain Market, Shantikunj Road, Haridwar.
