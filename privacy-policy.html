Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 27, 2026

This website is used to help patients contact Bhuvneshwari Dental Clinic & Implant Centre for appointments, online consultations, and treatment-related inquiries.

What information may be collected

When you use the booking forms or contact links, the website may collect your name, phone number, treatment interest, preferred date and time, and any concern you choose to share.

How the information is used

The clinic uses this information to respond to appointment requests, coordinate consultations, answer treatment questions, and improve patient communication.

Third-party tools

This website uses third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, and hosted web infrastructure. When you choose to contact the clinic through those services, their own privacy policies also apply.

Local device storage

For a lightweight and low-cost setup, this website may temporarily store basic event or lead data in your browser using local storage to support the booking flow.

Data sharing

The clinic does not present this website as a data-selling platform. Information shared through the site is intended only for clinic communication, appointment support, and treatment-related follow-up.

Your choices

You may contact the clinic to correct or request deletion of information you submitted through the website.
