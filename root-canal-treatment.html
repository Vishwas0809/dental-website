When do you need root canal treatment?

Root canal treatment may be needed when decay, cracks, trauma, or infection reaches the inner nerve space of a tooth. Warning signs can include lingering pain, sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling, pain while chewing, or a darkened tooth.

Is RCT painful?

Modern root canal treatment is very different from the old stories patients often hear. At Bhuvneshwari Dental Clinic, treatment is planned with local anesthesia, careful cleaning, and a gentle approach to reduce discomfort during the procedure.

What happens after treatment?

After infection is cleaned and sealed, the tooth usually needs a strong restoration or crown depending on its condition. Dr. Sharma explains the steps clearly before treatment begins.
